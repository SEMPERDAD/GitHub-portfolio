About

I build systems that are easy to reason about, maintain, and extend. My focus is on workflow orchestration — designing processes where each step is clear, reversible where possible, and verifiable before moving on. I believe the best engineering is invisible: it just works, and when it breaks, it breaks loudly and obviously.

I care deeply about minimal-impact changes, avoiding over-engineering, and capturing lessons so the same mistake never happens twice. Good systems are built iteratively, with honest feedback loops at every level.
