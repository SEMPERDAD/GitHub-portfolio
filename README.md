Use Cases
Use Case 1: Small Business Cybersecurity Audit
A local business has no formal IT security in place. CYTEC performs a threat assessment, identifies weak points in their network, and delivers a prioritized remediation plan — all without requiring enterprise-level resources.
Use Case 2: AI Voice Agent Deployment
A service-based business needs to handle after-hours client inquiries. CYTEC builds and deploys a custom ElevenLabs v3 conversational AI agent that answers FAQs, books appointments, and routes calls — running 24/7 with no human needed.
Use Case 3: Employee Onboarding Automation
A growing team struggles with inconsistent onboarding. CYTEC builds a Google Sheets + Apps Script pipeline that automatically assigns tasks, sends welcome emails via Resend API, and tracks completion — reducing onboarding time significantly.
Use Case 4: Senior Digital Literacy Program
A retirement community partners with CYTEC to run a 10-week course series. Residents learn to use smartphones, video call family members, recognize online scams, and navigate everyday apps — all in a relaxed, judgment-free environment.
Use Case 5: Network Setup & Optimization
A home-based business is experiencing slow speeds and frequent dropouts. CYTEC performs an on-site assessment, reconfigures the router and access points, and implements a solution that eliminates the issue and improves coverage throughout the space.

How We Work
Step 1: Intake & Assessment
Client reaches out via cytec-ai.com, email at info@cytec-ai.com, or via Blinq digital business card (NFC or QR scan). We assess the problem scope — remote or on-site.
Step 2: Prioritize
We identify the most critical needs first. Security vulnerabilities, productivity blockers, and automation opportunities are ranked by impact.
Step 3: Optimize
We implement clean, efficient solutions — no bloated software, no unnecessary complexity. Everything is tailored to the client's actual needs.
Step 4: Automate
Where possible, we build systems that run themselves — from onboarding flows to AI agents — so clients can focus on what matters.

Extending & Partnering
Add a new AI agent use case: Define the client scenario, configure the ElevenLabs v3 agent prompt, and connect it to the appropriate CRM or scheduling system.
Add a new training module: Follow the existing curriculum structure in training/curriculum/, include handout materials, and register the module in the program index.
Partner with a community organization: Retirement communities, libraries, and churches interested in hosting the Senior Digital Literacy program can reach out directly at info@cytec-ai.com.
Integrate with your business stack: CRM setup and onboarding automation can be adapted to most common platforms — reach out to discuss your specific toolchain.
