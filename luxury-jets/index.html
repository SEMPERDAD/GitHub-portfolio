Ch. 02 · Method

A brokerage with a dispatcher's discipline.

We operate on a tri-continental rotation. One phone line, one dispatcher, one authenticated itinerary.

  1. 01

    Brief

    A fifteen-minute call establishes route, party, cargo, and constraints. No forms, no portals, no logins.

  2. 02

    Match

    We source three airframes against your window, each with a named operator and a verified maintenance log attached.

  3. 03

    Dispatch

    Crew is notified, slot is filed, catering is built around your manifest. Median time from confirmation to wheels-up: three hours forty.

  4. 04

    Debrief

    A flight record arrives at your desk within twelve hours: duty-time, weather deviation, fuel burn, and anything we'd change.
