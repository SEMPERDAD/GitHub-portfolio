Ch. 04 · Recent work

Five ways we've been useful this year.

Shortened, sanitized, and paraphrased. The names we don't use are on purpose; the specifics are not.

  1. 01
    Cybersecurity audit

    A local firm, no IT lead, and a phishing scare.

    Threat assessment on a Tuesday, ranked remediation plan by Friday. MFA rolled out, password manager seeded, four risky SaaS logins retired. Insurer accepted the report as evidence of reasonable care.

  2. 02
    AI voice agent

    After-hours calls answered, appointments booked in their sleep.

    A service business was losing evening leads to voicemail. We shipped an ElevenLabs v3 agent that answers, qualifies, and books. It runs on its own and costs less than one part-time shift.

  3. 03
    Onboarding automation

    Day-one for new hires, without anyone remembering.

    A growing team had a checklist no-one followed. We replaced it with a Sheets + Apps Script pipeline that assigns tasks, sends welcome emails via Resend, and reports completion to the owner.

  4. 04
    Senior program

    Ten-week series at a retirement community in Palm Beach County.

    Twelve residents, two classes a week. By graduation, everyone could video-call family, identify a phishing text, and refill a prescription through the portal. We keep coming back.

  5. 05
    Network rebuild

    A home-based studio, dropouts on every client call.

    On-site survey, router retired, three access points in a mesh with a dedicated video-call VLAN. Zero dropouts since the callback.
